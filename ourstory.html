How It Began

The Origin Story

Rosmar Soap began as a personal journey. Rosemarie Tan Pamulaklakin, a proud Davaoeña, grew up watching her mother carefully tend to her skin using simple, natural ingredients passed down through generations. That tradition of self-care stayed with her — and eventually became her calling.

After years of struggling to find skincare products that were both effective and truly natural, Rosemarie decided to create her own. What started as small handcrafted batches made in her home kitchen quickly grew into something far greater — a brand trusted by thousands of Filipino women who want real results without harmful chemicals.

Today, Rosmar Soap is Rosemarie's proudest achievement — built not just with ingredients, but with heart, sacrifice, and an unshakeable belief that every woman deserves to glow.

"I made Rosmar for every woman who ever felt like she wasn't enough — because you are. And your skin deserves to feel that way too."

— Rosemarie Tan Pamulaklakin, Founder of Rosmar Soap

What We Stand For

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

Rosemarie Tan Pamulaklakin founded Rosmar Soap with one goal: to make safe, effective, and affordable skincare available to every Filipino woman. Every product is crafted with genuine care — not to sell a dream, but to deliver real results rooted in nature.

Our Vision

Rosemarie envisions Rosmar Soap as the go-to skincare brand of every proud Filipina — a brand that grows with its community, uplifts local talent, and proves that world-class skincare can come from right here in the Philippines.

Our Principles

What We Believe In

These are the principles Rosemarie built Rosmar Soap on — not just words, but the decisions she makes every single day.

Purity First

Rosemarie insists on using only ingredients that truly work. If it doesn't serve your skin, it doesn't go in the bottle — no fillers, no shortcuts.

Made with Love

"Rosmar" is Rosemarie's name — and her promise. Every product she formulates is tested with care and crafted with the kind of love only a founder who truly believes in her work can give.

Locally Rooted

Rosemarie is a proud Davaoeña, and Rosmar Soap reflects that pride. Made in Davao City, supporting local suppliers, and celebrating everything the Philippines has to offer.

Planet Responsible

Rosemarie believes in leaving the world better than she found it. Rosmar Soap uses recyclable packaging, a transparent supply chain, and formulas free from harmful chemicals.

Ingredient Philosophy

Only the Rarest, the Purest

Rosemarie personally oversees the selection of every ingredient that goes into a Rosmar product. She sources the finest natural actives available — from local Philippine botanicals rich in nutrients to trusted international ingredients known for their skin-loving properties.

Every ingredient is ethically sourced and rigorously tested before it ever touches your skin. Rosemarie is transparent about what she uses and why — because she believes you deserve to know exactly what you're putting on your face.

Under Rosemarie's standards, Rosmar Soap is cruelty-free, free from over 2,000 harmful chemicals, and continuously improved with every new formulation she develops.
