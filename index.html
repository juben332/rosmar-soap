Our Story

Where Luxury Meets
Pure Nature

Rosmar Soap is the creation of Rosemarie Tan Pamulaklakin — a proud Davaoeña who started this brand from the ground up with nothing but passion, hard work, and a dream to give every Filipino woman access to safe, effective, and beautiful skincare.

Every product Rosemarie creates is a love letter to your skin — formulated with the purest ingredients, free from harmful chemicals, and built on the belief that real beauty starts with taking care of yourself.

"I built Rosmar for you — the woman who works hard, who deserves to feel beautiful, and who should never have to choose between quality and safety." — Rosemarie
